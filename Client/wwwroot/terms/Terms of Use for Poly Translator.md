## 3. User Data Collection and Use

Poly Translator has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, age or gender. The only data it collects for the Author is:

- Diagnostic Data: Crash and error reports (error type, message, stack trace and the app component involved), app start and close events, app version, operating system version, display language, and a random installation identifier.

**Anonymous Diagnostics (Windows and Android):** The Windows and Android versions send diagnostic data to Microsoft Azure Application Insights to detect and fix crashes. The installation identifier is randomly generated and not linked to your name, email or device name. Microsoft derives an approximate location (city and country) from your IP address and does not store the full IP address. Error messages may incidentally contain file paths from your computer. Diagnostic data is retained for up to 90 days. Diagnostics are on by default; you can turn them off at any time in Settings with the "Send anonymous diagnostics" switch, after which no further diagnostic data is sent.

**Text Sent to Online Services:** To translate, Poly Translator sends the text you enter to online translation services (Google, Microsoft Bing and Yandex). To read a translation aloud, it sends the text to Google's text-to-speech service. To show related images, it sends the words you search to image search services (Google, Brave, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia). These services receive your IP address and handle the text under their own privacy policies; the Author does not operate them. On Windows, text captured from the screen is recognized on your device and then handled like typed text; the language data used for recognition is downloaded from GitHub.

**Optional AI Assistant:** If you connect an AI provider (OpenRouter, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic or a custom OpenAI-compatible server you enter), the AI chat sends the original text, the translated text and your messages to that provider using your own account, under that provider's terms. Nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you connect one. Your API key is stored only on your device in the system's secure storage, is sent only to the provider you chose, and is removed when you sign out of that provider or the provider rejects it. On Android the key is also excluded from cloud backups and device transfers. On Android, signing in to OpenRouter returns to the app through a static web page the Author hosts on Azure Static Web Apps (black-plant-00f5f8a0f.8.azurestaticapps.net), which receives the sign-in code and state in its address and only forwards them to the app, without running server-side code; the PKCE code verifier is never sent to that page, only directly to OpenRouter to obtain your key.

**Deleting Your Data:** Settings, AI keys and other data stored by the Software stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the Software or clear its storage. Diagnostic data is not linked to your name or email, so it cannot be matched to you and is deleted automatically after the retention period above; turning diagnostics off stops any further collection. To ask about or request deletion of other data held about you, contact <poly-help@outlook.com>.

**Reporting AI Replies:** You can report an AI reply you find harmful or wrong with the report button next to it. The report (the reason you pick, your optional comment, the reported reply and the provider and model that wrote it) is sent to the Author's Azure Application Insights so it can be reviewed, together with the diagnostic data above. Reports are sent even when diagnostics are off, because you send them on purpose, and are kept for up to 90 days.

**Use of Data:** The data above is used only to run, fix and improve the Software. The Author does **not sell** your data and does not share it with third parties except the service providers named in this section.
